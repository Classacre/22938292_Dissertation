====================================================================
 Expression-Matched Subsampling Validation - Summary
====================================================================

CONTEXT: The primary analysis uses the corrected noise score (standardized variance),
which already accounts for the mean-variance relationship. This subsampling
analysis serves as an independent, rigorous validation of those findings.

METHOD: We compare the corrected noise scores between gene groups that have
been explicitly matched for their median mean expression level.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
 Cahn (2016) Classification: gbM vs. Unmethylated
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Wilcoxon rank-sum test on corrected noise of matched sets:

	Wilcoxon rank sum test with continuity correction

data:  group1_data[[noise_col]] and group2_data[[noise_col]]
W = 13049016, p-value < 2.2e-16
alternative hypothesis: true location shift is not equal to 0


CONCLUSION:
A significant difference in corrected noise remains even after matching for
expression level. This strongly supports the conclusion that the association
is independent of mean expression.
